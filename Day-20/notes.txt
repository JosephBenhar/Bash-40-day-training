DAY 20 - UNTIL LOOPS

WHAT I LEARNED:
until works differently from while.

while:
TRUE = continue
FALSE = stop

until:
FALSE = continue
TRUE = stop

PRACTICE SCRIPTS:
retry.sh
serveronline.sh

EXAMPLE LOGIC:
attempt=1

until [ "$attempt" -gt 5 ]
do
    echo "Retry $attempt"
    attempt=$((attempt + 1))
done

The loop keeps running UNTIL the condition becomes true.

MEMORY:
WHILE = KEEP GOING WHILE TRUE
UNTIL = KEEP GOING UNTIL TRUE
