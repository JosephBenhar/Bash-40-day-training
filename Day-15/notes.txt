DAY 15 - LOGICAL OR

WHAT I LEARNED:
|| means OR.

With OR, at least ONE condition must be true.

EXAMPLE:
role="admin"

if [ "$role" = "admin" ] || [ "$role" = "security" ]
then
    echo "Access granted"
fi

The condition succeeds if:
role is admin
OR
role is security.

MEMORY:
&& = BOTH
|| = AT LEAST ONE
