DAY 10 - NUMERIC EQUALITY

WHAT I LEARNED:
-eq checks whether two numbers are equal.

EXAMPLE:
number=5

if [ "$number" -eq 5 ]
then
    echo "Numbers are equal"
fi

IMPORTANT:
-eq is used for numeric equality.

= can be used when comparing text.

EXAMPLES:
[ "$number" -eq 5 ]
[ "$name" = "Joseph" ]

MEMORY:
-eq = EQUAL NUMBERS
