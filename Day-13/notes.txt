DAY 13 - LESS THAN OR EQUAL

WHAT I LEARNED:
-le means less than or equal to.

EXAMPLE:
attempts=5

if [ "$attempts" -le 5 ]
then
    echo "Attempts are within the limit"
fi

Both of these would be TRUE:
3 -le 5
5 -le 5

NUMERIC COMPARISON MEMORY:
-gt = greater than
-lt = less than
-eq = equal
-ne = not equal
-ge = greater than or equal
-le = less than or equal
