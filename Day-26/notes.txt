DAY 26 - SORT AND UNIQ

sort
Arranges lines.

uniq
Removes duplicate lines that are next to each other.

sort file | uniq
Sorts first, then removes duplicates.

uniq -c
Counts repeated lines.

SOC Example:
sort failed_ips.txt | uniq -c

Result:
2 10.0.0.5
3 185.10.20.30
2 192.168.1.10

Finding:
185.10.20.30 appeared 3 times.

Troubleshooting:
Blank lines are also counted as data.
cat -n filename shows line numbers and helped identify blank lines.

MEMORY:
SORT -> PASS -> COUNT
