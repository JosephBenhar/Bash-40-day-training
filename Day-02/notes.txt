DAY 02 - COMMENTS

WHAT I LEARNED:
Comments are notes inside a Bash script.

SYNTAX:
# This is a comment

Bash ignores lines beginning with # when they are comments.

WHY COMMENTS MATTER:
They help me remember what my code does.
They also make scripts easier for other people to understand.

EXAMPLE:
#!/usr/bin/env bash

# Display a welcome message
echo "Welcome Joseph"

MEMORY:
# = NOTE FOR HUMANS
